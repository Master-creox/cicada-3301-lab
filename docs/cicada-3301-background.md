# Cicada 3301 — the real background (and what is *not* real)

## What happened
On 4 January 2012 an anonymous image appeared on 4chan: a cicada, the
words *"We are looking for highly intelligent individuals"*, and the
signature **3301**. Inside the image, steganography (OutGuess) hid a
message that led — through a Caesar cipher, Reddit, a book cipher and
a phone number — to more images and more puzzles. New rounds followed
in 2013 and 2014.

The puzzles mixed disciplines like no recruitment test before them:
steganography, classical ciphers, prime numbers, Mayan numerals,
Anglo-Saxon runes, William Blake, medieval music, and physical posters
with QR codes in ~14 cities worldwide (the Warsaw poster photo in this
project's inspiration set is one of the real ones).

Every official message was **PGP-signed**. That signature is the only
authenticity test: anything "new" from 3301 without it is fake.

## Where the trail ends
The 2014 round produced **Liber Primus** — a book written in runes
using the *Gematria Primus* alphabet (29 runes, prime values). A few
pages are solved, including one that reads:

> "Some wisdom: the primes are sacred. The totient function is sacred.
> All things should be encrypted. Know this."

…and the page *"An End"*, which uses a totient stream cipher. **Most
of Liber Primus is still unsolved** — a decade later, nobody has
publicly cracked the remaining pages. The last verified signed message
(2016) said, in effect: *Liber Primus is the way.* Then silence.

## What the viral images get wrong
- The circulating "TIBERIVS CAESAR — I found the city of the
  immortals" extraction is **not** a verified 3301 message; it comes
  from fan/ARG videos. The real first hidden message was a
  Caesar-shifted pointer, not a prophecy.
- Map collages with numbered points (e.g. on the Urbano Monte map)
  are fan theories, not canon.
- Cicada never recruited publicly, sold anything, or claimed to be
  aliens/time travellers. Whoever ran it — agency, collective, or
  artists — never confirmed anything.

## Why this lab exists
CicadaLab rebuilds the *techniques* of the solved puzzles as working,
tested tools — and wraps them in a small hunt of its own. It is an
educational tribute. It is **not** affiliated with 3301, and it does
not pretend to solve Liber Primus. If you crack that, don't tell us —
tell the world, with a PGP signature.

*Patience is a virtue.*
